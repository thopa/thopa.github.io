Gregal — Virtual

a pre-wired modulation web you can patch, running in your browser Gregal 1.0.11

The PATCH window (the device's own window script). Click an input, then an output, to patch it.

Push: rows 1-8 from the top, pads 1-8 from the left. Tap = click; hold = keep the mouse down.

Shift, Select and Delete stay down until you click them again (one mouse). Shift + Select = the random page; Shift + Delete = init (every pad white, click any pad).

120 bpm

A small synth stands in for your instrument: lane 1 moves its filter, lane 2 its pan, lane 3 its level, lane 4 its pitch (± 3 st). In Live a lane moves any parameter you map.

What runs here: Gregal's own release engine script (patching, rnd new / vary / drift, init, undo, slots, the Push pad logic), its own PATCH window script, and the JavaScript mirror of its gen~ core that the build generates from the same source and tests against it. Not here: Live itself (no mapping to other devices: the four lanes drive the small synth), the Push colour menu, the floating window's resizing and the knob pages on Push's screen (the box under the pads shows their names). Pad colours are close to the device's, not exact.
