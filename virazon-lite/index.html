How Virazon Lite moves

Virazón is the Spanish name for the sea breeze that turns round with the day. Virazon Lite is a melody machine built on one idea: sixteen bits go round in a loop, and the first few are read as a note. Write decides how often a new bit comes in: at 0 the loop is locked and repeats, a little and it drifts, a lot and it never repeats. Six voices read the same loop from different places, so one small pattern becomes a line, its echoes, a harmony and an answer. Each stage below runs the device's own engine script and has the device's own controls.

01THE REGISTER→ every voice

The top row is the loop as it is now: lit = 1. Click a bit to flip it. Underneath, the last steps, newest on the right: a locked loop makes a repeating pattern, Write makes it smear. The coral bar is the window the main voice reads as a note; the small marks are where the three taps read. Length sets how many bits go round (2 to 16), Rate how fast, Feedback how the bit coming back in is made, Source and Chaos what a new bit is, and Lean tilts new bits towards 1 or 0.

TryPlay with Write at 0: the melody repeats every Length steps. Raise Write to 20 % for a few bars, then back to 0: whatever you hear at that moment is kept.

02READ → PITCH→ voices

How the window becomes a note. binary reads the bits as a number, count counts the ones, sum gives every bit its own pitch (in scale degrees) and adds the lit ones. The result is fitted to the Scale and Root, above Base, inside Range.

TryRead = sum, Write 0. Change a few of the Sum pitches: the same rhythm, a new melody, still in key.

03FEEL→ timing

Gate is the note length against the step. Swing delays every second step. Chance scales every voice's own chance at once: at 0 nothing plays while the loop keeps moving.

TryChance at 50 %: half the notes drop out, the register still turns, and the line gets air.

04VOICES→ MIDI notes

V1 is the main line. T1-T3 are taps: they read the same loop further along (Position) and move it in the scale (Transpose), so they play V1's melody later and elsewhere. H (harmony) plays the higher or lower of V1 and the notes coming in on the track. P (parrot) answers: it plays N steps, rests M. Each voice has its own velocity, chance and rhythm: every step, Euclidean (steps, hits, rotate) or whenever a chosen bit of the register is 1. Pick a voice to see its controls; the tab colours are the colours in What you hear.

TrySwitch T1 on, Position +3, Transpose 2: a canon two degrees up. Then give P Euclid 5 of 8 and let it answer.

05SOURCES→ lanes · not in Lite

Four more registers turn beside the main one, each with its own Length, Write, Freeze and clock Divider. They make no notes: they are what the lanes read.

TryR1 at Divider /4 with Write 30 %, sent by lane 1 to Range: the melody opens and closes every few bars.

06LANES→ anywhere · not in Lite

A lane reads a source (a register, a voice, a random value) and sends it, scaled by Depth and moved by Offset, to one of Virazon Lite's own controls. In Live, map sends it to any parameter of any device in the Set or to the mixer, with Glide; here map only shows its meter.

TryLane 1 on, source R1, destination write, Depth 40 %: one register decides when the main loop changes.

07PATTERNS AND RANDOM→ everything

A pattern keeps the loop's bits and every setting above. Click a slot to recall it; press store, then a slot, to keep yours. Chain walks through slots every few beats or bars. The dice: new gives fresh values, vary stays near the pattern stored last, drift takes a small step from where things are; Rnd Amount sets how far. Write, Rate, the key, Base and the voices' on / off are never rolled.

TryPress drift a few times while it plays: the line walks. Like it? store, then slot 2. Then chain 1 to 2 every 2 bars.

08PUSH PADSrows 1-8 from the top

The pads as Push lights them. Rows 1-2 are the loop's bits, row 7 the voices (pad 8: freeze), row 8 the patterns; the column on the right is the scene buttons (patterns 1-8). Tap = press, hold a pad for a fifth of a second = its knob page. Shift, Select and Delete latch here because a mouse has one finger: Shift + Select = the random page, Shift + Delete = init.

09WHAT YOU HEARsix voices

The notes of every voice, scrolling right to left. Incoming notes stands in for a melody on another track: the harmony voice H needs one (in Live, set the track's MIDI From).
