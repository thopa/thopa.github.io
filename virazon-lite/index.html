Push pads

Rows 1 to 8 from the top, pads 1 to 8 from the left. The column on the right is the eight scene buttons, 1 at the top: patterns 1 to 8 (bright = the pattern playing, grey = stored, dark = empty): click to recall, hold half a second to store, Delete + click to clear. Shift, Select and Delete latch here, because a mouse has one finger: click once to hold, again to let go. Click a pad = tap. Press a pad for longer than a fifth of a second = hold. With the keyboard: Tab to a pad, Space or Enter; keep the key down to hold.

Not on this page: the pad colour menu (Shift), the incoming notes for the harmony voice, the modulation lanes and the pattern chain's timing against Live's bars. © thopa 2026.
