Devices

Network

Eight modulators that move each other.

Eight nodes (LFO, slope, sample and hold, Geiger, slew, random, chaos) wire into each other's rate, amp and shape, so the whole web keeps changing. Each node moves up to three parameters anywhere in Live, 24 in all, and can hand its movement from one to the next each cycle. Eight patterns chain and morph.

Live 11 & 12 · Push 3 · modulation · 24 lanes · morph

Try it in the browser Network 15 €

Galerna

Sixteen bands. Every one moves your Set.

A bank of sixteen filters, each with its own level and its own envelope follower. Any band can drive any parameter in Live, so the kick band opens a reverb or the top band opens a delay. Eight curves morph on the beat, and a vocoder needs no second track.

Live 11 & 12 · Push 3 · audio effect · 17 followers · vocoder

Try it in the browser Galerna 15 €

Virazón

A looping shift register, steered.

Sixteen bits go round in a loop and are read as a melody. Write sets how often a new bit comes in, from locked to never repeating. Six voices read the same loop from different places; lanes and a second set of registers move the rest.

Live 11 & 12 · Push 3 · six voices · lanes · morph

Try it in the browser Virazón 15 € Virazón Lite free

Abrego

Two sequences, five voices.

Two sequences of different lengths play against each other, so the pattern only repeats when both line up again. Accumulators shift the notes on every pass. Abrego Voice sends each of the five voices to its own track.

Live 11 & 12 · Push 3 · five voices · accumulators · morph

Try it in the browser Abrego 15 € Abrego Lite free

Cierzo Lite

Diatonic chords from Push 3 pads.

Every pad plays a chord that fits the key. Hints show what usually comes next; modifier pads change voicing, inversion and extensions; borrowed chords step outside the key. With Push 3 and Live 12, MPE moves the whole chord.

Live 11 & 12 · Push 3, any 8×8 grid or a keyboard · 35 scales · MPE

Try it in the browser Cierzo Lite free
