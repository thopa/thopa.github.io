How Cierzo Lite moves

Cierzo is the north-west wind of the Ebro valley. Cierzo Lite turns one pad into a chord built from your key, so every pad you press fits. This page runs the device's own script, the same file that ships inside the .amxd: what the pads light, name and play here is what they do on Push in Live. The sound is a small built-in synth.
